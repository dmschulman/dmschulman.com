## Building
Slowly but surely getting the basement project accomplished. Not only is it tedious work with smelly oil-based paint but my neck is angled up at all times which as you can imagine makes it hard to power through.

Our house is over a hundred years old so rusty screws, metal hangers, and other bizarre bits of hardware protrude from the joists. All must be removed and any remaining holes must be patched before painting.

Similarly, I've slowly and methodically been reworking my 2 main eurorack modular cases that I bring out to shows. As I rework these cases so to does my philosophy on assembling a system and the method to achieving the goal. Not only will I be capturing these thoughts when I put together my Studio section of this website but I'm also going to have an area dedicated to showcasing and explaining some of these modular rigs.

## Thinking about
The Jewish new year, Rosh Hashanah, is fast approaching. I always disliked the high holy days as a kid as it meant sitting in long synagogue services and at a time when school was also kicking into full gear.

Now as an adult, while I don't necessarily count down until the two holiest days on the Jewish calendar, I do find more opportunity to reflect and take stock. I also appreciate how lucky it is that we can celebrate 2 new years every year, and with it, the chance to set new goals and make resolutions (if you're in to that sort of thing, that is).

In the same vein, I'm taking stock of what the next milestones for this website entail. With the recent launch my <a href="/words">Words</a> section it is now incumbent on me to transition over all my old blog posts as well as finish writing and publish any delinquent content that I've neglected the last little while.

Also planning to round out the <a href="/work">Work</a> section in the same way before moving on to more ambitious projects.
