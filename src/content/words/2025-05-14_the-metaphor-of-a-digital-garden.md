Metaphor is key to the concept of a "[digital garden](https://github.com/MaggieAppleton/digital-gardeners)". From the name alone we can understand it to be some sort of plot that individuals cultivate with their own knowledge and expertise. Ordering and organizing information is uniquely human, a compulsion that's been with us long before the internet, but a compulsion uniquely complimented by the basic concept of the internet.

Unlike more formal systems like a wiki, a paradigm that's been finely tuned and vetted by billions over 30+ years, the purpose and structure of a digital garden is as unique and opinionated as the gardener themself. So when it comes to setting up my own system I'll be taking some inspiration from what's already out there, but a lot will shift and change as I cement the structural ideas and really get to the bottom of how one easily sorts and collates lots of loose thoughts and ideas.

This is, again, where the metaphor of gardening comes in handy. Plants, animals, single-celled organisms, and others all have scientific classifications that order themselves along a [taxonomic ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxonomic_rank). While 8 orders of magnitude might be a little overkill for a small plot of digital farmland, there's something to the concept of content areas living inside of content areas living inside of content areas.

Trees are also a handy metaphor (and incidentally are all over the place when it comes to computing). You have your main trunk, representing the collection itself, on to which hundreds of branches (individual topic areas) reach out from. Those individual branches have other branches (subtopics) coming off of them, and then finally those other branches have an infinite amount of leaves, flowers, seed pods, and whathaveyou (your little bits of information).

None of this is new information to people interested in cataloging and organizing all the world's stuff, but these are elegant ideas to keep in mind.

Also important to note that none of this helps me address the actual method through which these individual bits of information will all link through to each other and not to items outside of the topic area, or otherwise, how I digitally tag and represent this big web (metaphor!) of stuff.
