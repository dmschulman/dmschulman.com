Blogging is rough. Good ideas come and go, but there's always an urge to turn a small thought into a long essay. Sometimes this is successful, but other times I just want to get something on the page, quickly. I also don't want to pester an audience (do I have an audience?) with small disjointed interstitial thoughts when they've likely come here for something else.

So, behold the microblog! Maybe I'll just call it a blog in the end (longer form writing ends up categorized as "pages" or "posts") but this is my little foray into writing for public consumption again.
