I absolutely struggle with putting words on the page when the words are about me specifically. From imposter syndrome to feeling embarrassment over potentially penning a weak sentence, emotions run high when I'm ask to describe myself.

Am I being honest enough? Am I overselling myself? Are people going to appreciate reading this? All these thoughts and more wiz through my brain as I attempt to what will eventually become a personal statement on my site's About section. You could publish a book out of all the paragraphs I've written about myself (and subsequently tossed) over the years.

But work is work, the show must go on...
