Or rather, can be! Which is exciting to think about (only partially because it gives me some ideas for improving this "microblog").

One thing I hope to capture in this new site is some kind of "digital garden". Briefly, a [digital garden](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history/) is a paradigm where every bit of information you create on your website can be catalogued and connected in some fashion, centralizing all your data in a more meaningful way.

I blog about cooking, and I have a collection of recipes, and I also post random things I've learned about gardening. Tag all that information in some fashion and suddenly I can build a repository of cooking information and plan my garden around my favorite things to prepare in the kitchen. Not a perfect analogy, but you get the idea.

With that, I should likely consider tagging these small posts and formatting for keywords so the useful information I capture here can be used across that ecosystem.

But am I just getting one step closer to building a blog in this space? Maybe.
